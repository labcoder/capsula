# The dock

The dock sits at the top center of your screen's work area. It shows Starting, Ready,
Reconnecting or Offline. Move the pointer over it to open the small status panel; move away
from both the dock and panel to close it. Hovering doesn't take focus from your current app.

Use the tray menu to choose one of nine positions: top, middle or bottom, aligned left,
center or right. This choice currently lasts until the shell restarts. A later release moves
it into Settings → General and saves it.

If the taskbar or display work area changes, choose the position again to realign the dock.
Display-scale changes currently require restarting the shell.

If the background process can't be reached, the panel and tray offer Retry. Quit in either
the panel or tray shuts down both processes. Closing the panel alone doesn't quit.

While you hold [the shortcut](shortcut.md), the dock shows Listening. A double tap opens a small
typing box under the dock; [Esc](escape.md) hides it. Chat, threads and settings controls appear
as those features become available; the panels don't offer commands that can't yet work.
